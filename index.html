    Why I built it, what changed, what was hard

    Why

    A small People team at a scale-up spends much of its week on repeatable work: balance lookups, payroll questions, offboarding checklists, chasing replies. I wanted that capacity back for the human parts of the job.

    I also wanted to test a bigger idea: that an AI-native People function treats agents as teammates with a role, scope and manager, not as a chatbot bolted onto a portal.

    Impact so far

    • Routine requests get an answer in minutes instead of waiting in a shared inbox.
    • Follow-ups stop slipping, because chasing is Deco's job, not a sticky note.
    • The humans on the team review proposals rather than drafting from scratch.
    • It became the template for how we design further agents: role, scope, guardrails, reviewer.

    The hardest part

    The model was the easy bit. Drawing the line between propose and act was hard: which actions can run unattended, which need sign-off, and which must never be touched by an agent at all.

    Getting that wrong in HR is costly: wrong pay, a mishandled grievance, a decision about a person made by software. Under the EU AI Act and GDPR Art. 22 that line is also a legal one.

    What Deco runs on

    Deco is the newest piece of an AI-native People stack I built over the past year, mostly hands-on. Each piece came out of a real bottleneck.

    Employee-facing

    People Assistant

    A Slack bot grounded in our policy wiki. Answers by country (DE, UK, US, CA), routes payroll questions to the right provider, and hard-escalates anything sensitive.

    Plumbing

    Custom HR system connectors

    MCP servers I wrote for our HRIS and applicant tracking system, so agents get the fields they need with scoped access, beyond what native connectors expose.

    Payroll

    Reconciliation agent

    Compares HR data with payroll exports across countries and flags mismatches before pay runs, for a human to resolve.

    Insight

    People analytics & org planning

    Dashboards for headcount, attrition and org design, built with AI and fed from live HR data instead of monthly spreadsheets.

    Hiring

    Recruiting automation

    Hiring dashboards, automated pipeline steps and AI-assisted interview scorecards, with humans making every hiring call.

    Talent

    Talent framework, rebuilt

    Our 4-grid talent framework redesigned with AI support, so calibration starts from structured evidence.

    The operating rules

    AI proposes, humans decide

    Anything touching pay, contracts or an individual's situation is drafted, then approved by a person.

    Hard escalation

    Grievances, health, conflict and anything sensitive go straight to a human. Deco does not draft a reply.

    Least privilege

    Scoped, read-first access to each HR system. Write actions are explicit and logged.

    Country-aware

    Answers follow the employee's jurisdiction and the right payroll route, not a single global policy.
